Tentative run of show

Times are tentative.

  1. 9:30 AM
    Bride’s family prepares

    The bride’s family gets ready in the hall. Check the PA system and microphone, assign seats and hold the final briefing.

  2. 10:00 AM
    Welcome the groom’s family

    The bride’s family welcomes the groom’s family into the hall. Collect their gifts and place them on the gift table.

    Everyone takes their seats.

    The emcee opens the ceremony using the microphone and refers to the short emcee script.

  3. 10:10 AM
    Family introductions and discussion

    Introduce family members and start the discussion. Ibu requested an informal session without a microphone.

  4. 10:35 AM
    Gift exchange

    Exchange gifts. Ask three people from each side to take part. Akak, Kak Mija and Kak Elaine represent the bride’s side for the exchange.

  5. 10:45 AM
    Ring ceremony

    Invite the bride in.

  6. 10:55 AM
    Couple and family photos

    Take photos using the photography moodboard as a reference.

  7. 11:00 AM
    Meal service

    Gastrohub staff serve the meal. Kak Mija invites everyone to eat.

  8. 12:00 PM
    Mingling and group photos

    Mingling and photos. Tentative: invite the groom in after 11 AM and continue taking photos.

  9. 1:00 PM
    Event ends
